    Streamer permissions

    Ask the streamer first, or check whether they already allow clipping, and save their reply here. The Publisher warns you before you post a channel with no permission on record.

      The honest part

      • The footage isn't yours. The game belongs to its publisher and the stream to the streamer. Ask before you post their clips.
      • Plain reposts can lose the money. YouTube's reused-content policy reviews clips and compilations. Add commentary or your own angle (the Voiceover tool is there for this).
      • Copyright claims are separate. A streamer or publisher can still claim or take down a clip.
      • Views can pay, but nothing is guaranteed. Being early and adding your own take is the edge.
